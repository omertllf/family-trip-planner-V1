1 — Trip Dashboard & Table View

Central hub showing live cost totals per category (Stays, Flights, Cars, Activities) and a sortable, searchable table of all bookings. Columns are drag-reorderable. Duplicate entries are auto-detected and highlighted in amber. Currency switches between USD, EUR and ILS using live exchange rates.
